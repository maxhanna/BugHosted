How to Play
  • The highlighted center row is the pitch line — it slides side to side, and each column slides up/down.
  • ◀ / ▶ (or ←/→) shift the whole center row; marbles wrap around.
  • Pick a column (tap it, or press 1–5) and use ▲ / ▼ (or ↑/↓) to cycle that column's marbles up or down — or just drag the column up/down from any row.
  • Line up 3+ same-colored marbles touching in any row or column — they pop, the ones above fall down, and the fall can start chain matches.
  • A special color is hot each game: popping marbles of that color fills your reserve.
  • Every match dumps garbage onto your opponent — 3-in-a-row sends 1 marble, 4 sends 2, 5+ sends 3.
  • New marbles rain in from the top — you lose when your columns fill up.
