Lose Your Marbles

🔮 Marbles

Drop marbles one at a time. Match 3+ same-colored marbles to pop them — every match of 3 sends 1 marble raining onto your opponent, 4 sends 2, 5 sends 3. First to fill their board loses!

Rooms work across computers — share the 4-letter code. Works on mobile & desktop.

How to Play
  • Marbles drop one at a time. Click a column (or press 1–6, or arrows + space) to drop yours.
  • When 3 or more same-colored marbles touch, they pop.
  • Every popped group of size N sends N − 2 marbles raining onto your opponent's board (3 → 1, 4 → 2, 5 → 3).
  • Sent marbles land at random columns — they can cascade your opponent into trouble, or help them.
  • You lose when your columns fill to the top. Keep yours low and bury your rival!
