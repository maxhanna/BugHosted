How to Play
  • The highlighted center row is the pitch line — the only place matches count.
  • ◀ / ▶ (or ←/→) shift the whole center row; marbles wrap around.
  • Pick a column (tap it, or press 1–6) and use ▲ / ▼ (or ↑/↓) to cycle that column's marbles up or down.
  • Slide 3+ same-colored marbles side by side in the center row — they pop and the ones above fall down.
  • A special color is hot each game: popping marbles of that color fills your reserve.
  • Pop a 5+ match to dump your reserve onto your opponent's board.
  • New marbles rain in from the top — you lose when your columns fill up.
