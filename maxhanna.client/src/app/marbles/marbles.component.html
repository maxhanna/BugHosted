How to Play
  • Move the glowing cursor along the center row with ← / → — or tap a marble / press 1–5 — to pick your handle.
  • ↑ / ↓ (or drag up/down on mobile) shifts the selected marble's whole column up/down; a column can't shift past the top or bottom (marbles never wrap).
  • Space rotates the whole center row right (or drag it sideways on mobile; ◀ / ▶ on-screen arrows shift it too).
  • Line up 3+ same-colored marbles in an unbroken horizontal run across the center row — they pop, the columns re-compact, and the fall can start chain matches.
  • A special color is hot each game: popping marbles of that color fills your reserve.
  • Every match dumps garbage onto your opponent — 3-in-a-row sends 1 marble, 4 sends 2, 5 sends 3.
  • Line up all 5 in the center row and your whole reserve dumps onto the opponent at once.
  • New marbles rain in from the top — you lose when your columns fill up.
  • Local 2P: P1 takes the bottom board with ←/→/↑/↓ + Space; P2 takes the top board with A/D (select), W/S (shift column) and F (rotate the row) — like the original 1997 shared-keyboard mode.
