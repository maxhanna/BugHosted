Movie Player Help

🎬 Movie Player Features

🔹 Adding Movies / TV Shows

To add a video to the public list:

  1. Enter the movie or episode title
  2. Either:
    • Paste a YouTube URL (full episodes, trailers, movies), OR
    • Pick a video file from the file explorer using the media selector
  3. Click the 💾 save button or press Enter

🔹 Playing Videos

  • Click any title in the list to play it
  • YouTube videos play in the embedded player
  • Uploaded files play in the video player
  • Click ◼ to expand fullscreen, 🛑 to stop playback
  • Click ⚡ to play a random video
  • Click 🕸 to open the current YouTube video in a new tab

🔹 Public Playlists

  • Every playlist is public. Anyone can browse and play anyone else's list from the "Playlist (public)" dropdown — no login or share links needed.
  • The dropdown shows the owner's name next to each playlist.
  • Only the owner of a playlist can edit, rename, or delete it.
  • Create a new playlist with ➕ and add videos to it with ✏️ → checkboxes → 💾Save.

🔹 Managing Videos

  • Edit: Click ✏️ to edit a title, then 💾 to save
  • Delete: Click ❌ to remove a video
  • Search: Use the search box or the YouTube search (▶️) button
  • Sort: Newest / Oldest / Alphanumeric / Random

Note: You must be logged in to add or edit videos.
